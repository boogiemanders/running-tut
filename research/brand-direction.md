# Running Tut brand direction

Working notes on what Running Tut is going for. Started 2026-11-06 from Anders and Tut's conversations and the [competitor research](2026-11-competitor-sentiment.md).

## Positioning

**Luxury that lasts, with pockets.** Designer-level style at a premium price, built to hold up, with storage that actually works.

## Product principles

- **Durability is part of the luxury.** The main complaint about Satisfy, the luxury leader, is that pieces rip, shrink or yellow and support offers a coupon. Running Tut should be the premium brand that doesn't fall apart.
- **Real pockets.** Phone-sized, bounce-free, reachable by feel. Big pockets in running pants are a priority (Tut's point; full-length running pants with real storage are the least-served slot found).
- **Gel loops.** Loops or holders for gels, alongside pockets, so runners can carry fuel without a belt.
- **Restrained, considered colorways.** Anders doesn't like Hoka's colorways, and Hoka's loud, bright look is also the most criticized aesthetic among the brands researched. Lean toward Bandit-style clean, streetwear-led design.

## Open questions

- Price point: Bandit tier (~$80 to $120 shorts) or Satisfy tier ($200+)?
- Which first product proves the thesis (likely running pants with big pockets and gel loops)?
- What durability promise or warranty backs up "lasts"?
